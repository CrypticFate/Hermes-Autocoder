goals over maintenance scans. Require evidence and acceptance criteria, reject
cycles, deduplicate plans and wait for merged prerequisites. Cache unchanged
scans. Render plans alongside implementation PRs.

Acceptance: valid graph persistence, duplicate suppression, no invented feature
authorization, no planner code edits, and idle behavior for empty plans.
